# hybrid v1 (season 2, from 2026-11-16)

bold's ambition with careful's caution. At 10:15, 11:00, 12:00, 13:00 and 14:00 ET, on bars before that minute, it keeps only stocks that are holding up (above today's open, VWAP so far and yesterday's close) and buys the biggest expected movers first (forecast sigma, as bold ranks them). Limit buy at the last price +0.05%, cancelled at the next checkpoint if unfilled. Target **+3%** from the limit price; stop at today's low so far, but at least 1% and at most 3% below the entry. Sized so a stop loses at most 0.66% of the account (34% per stock, up to 100% of cash): at most 3 filled trades a day, so a day where every stop hits loses about 2%. Flat by 15:55 ET.
